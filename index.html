About Our Institution

Welcome to Darul Falah Model Madrasah

Darul Falah Model Madrasah is an academic institution committed to quality education, discipline, character development and moral values.

We believe that education is not only about academic achievement. It is also about developing responsible, confident and principled individuals who can contribute positively to society.
